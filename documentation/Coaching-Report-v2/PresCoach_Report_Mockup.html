This reads as an enthusiastic feature tour rather than a case for why the technology matters. The gap is not competence, it is craft. You know the material cold, the energy is real, and the enunciation is excellent. What the audience never gets is a reason to care before the features start.

Three things to change before the next recording

Three habits are doing most of the damage, and they share causes. There is no story anywhere in the five minutes, so Engagement, Persuasion, and Executive presence all take the same hit from the same place. The softening language runs underneath an otherwise authoritative delivery and takes the edge off it. And the talk has no roadmap at the front and no conclusion at the back, which is why the content lands but does not stick. These are three fixes, not thirty. Everything else in this report is refinement on top of them.
